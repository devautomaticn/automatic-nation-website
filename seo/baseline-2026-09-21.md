## The most expensive miss

**`airtable consultants` — 210 searches/month, $30.36 CPC, position 32.**

That is the agency's own service, the term carries genuine buying intent, and
the page Google has chosen to rank for it is
`/how-to-hire-and-where-to-find-the-right-airtable-consultant/` — a blog post
that teaches the reader how to evaluate consultants in general. The actual
service page, `/lp/airtable-consulting/`, ranks for **nothing**.

`airtable experts` ($33.57 CPC) sits at 54 behind the same post.

Roughly $6,400/month of equivalent paid clicks sits behind two keywords the site
is already halfway to, answered by the wrong URL. Fixing which page targets
what is cheaper than any amount of new content, and it is the first thing this
pipeline should be pointed at.

## How this was measured

`dataforseo_labs_google_domain_rank_overview` and
`dataforseo_labs_google_relevant_pages` were read directly. The 300-row keyword
pull was too large to read in full, so it was aggregated with `jq` into
`keyword-map.tsv` and every figure in the "Where those rankings sit" and
"Intent split" tables is computed from that complete file rather than from a
sampled read.
