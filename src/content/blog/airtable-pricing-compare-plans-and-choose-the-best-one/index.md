## What Does “Per Seat” Mean in Airtable Pricing?

Airtable charges per seat, meaning per user with editing rights. Here’s how it works:

- The **Free plan** allows up to 5 editors per base at no cost.
- On **Team** and **Business** plans, you pay for every editor user.
- Read-only collaborators, form submitters, or share link users don’t count.
- If you add editors mid-billing cycle, charges adjust based on their active days.

This pricing approach helps scale Airtable efficiently as your team grows—you pay only for the seats you use.

## Choosing the Best Airtable Pricing Plan for Your Needs

### Free Plan: Ideal for Starters and Small Projects

The Free plan suits those exploring Airtable or managing small projects. Features include:

- Unlimited bases
- Up to 5 editors
- 1,000 records and 1 GB attachments per base
- 100 automation runs monthly
- 500 AI credits per editor

Perfect if your team is small and your needs are simple.

### Team Plan: For Growing Collaborative Teams

The Team plan supports larger teams requiring enhanced collaboration:

- 50,000 records and 20 GB attachments per base
- 25,000 automation runs monthly
- 15,000 AI credits per user
- Gantt charts, Timeline views, extensions, and standard syncs
- Optional paid Portals for external data sharing

Best for teams coordinating bigger projects without enterprise-level needs.

### Business Plan: Advanced Features for Power Users

Designed for teams needing enhanced automation, security, and governance, the Business plan offers:

- 125,000 records and 100 GB attachments per base
- 100,000 automation runs monthly
- 20,000 AI credits per user
- Premium and two-way syncs, verified data, admin panel, SSO, and executive views
- Portals add-on for advanced partnership or client collaboration

Ideal to boost productivity with Airtable Automations and governance.

### Enterprise Plan: Tailored for Large Organizations

The Enterprise plan caters to big companies with complex requirements:

- 500,000 records and 1 TB attachments per base
- Scaled automation runs and AI credits
- Enterprise API access, audit logs, HyperDB, data loss prevention (DLP)
- Custom pricing with dedicated support

Perfect for organizations building critical applications or deeply integrating Airtable into their workflows. Airtable Enterprise plans will usually start at around $10,000 per year.

## Tips to Optimize Your Airtable Pricing Plan

- Evaluate your team size carefully; pay only for editors, while others collaborate for free.
- Monitor automation runs to avoid exceeding limits.
- Keep track of record and attachment quotas to prevent overages.
- Use Airtable’s AI features strategically for efficient credit usage.

## Final Thoughts on Airtable Pricing

Airtable pricing is flexible, letting you scale from an individual user to a large organization. Whether you choose the Free or Enterprise plan, understanding these options helps you select the right plan for your budget and goals.
