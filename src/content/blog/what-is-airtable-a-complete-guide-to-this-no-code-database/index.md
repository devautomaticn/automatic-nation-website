## What is Airtable? Understanding This No-Code Relational Database

At its core, [Airtable is a **no-code relational database**](http://airtable.com/) that helps you organize data visually and intuitively. Unlike traditional spreadsheets, Airtable allows you to create relationships between tables. This makes your data smarter and more connected — all without requiring any coding knowledge.

Imagine the ease of Excel or Google Sheets combined with the power and functionality of a database. That’s what Airtable offers. It’s perfect for users who want more control over their data without the complexity of managing technical database systems.

## Why Is Airtable Called “Spreadsheets on Steroids”?

Traditional spreadsheets treat data as isolated rows and columns, which makes managing linked information challenging. Airtable takes the familiar grid format and enhances it with powerful database features such as:

- **Linked records** between tables, similar to relational databases
- Various **field types** like attachments, checkboxes, dropdown menus, and barcodes
- Custom **views** including Kanban boards, calendars, galleries, and grids
- Real-time collaboration with team members

With Airtable, your data becomes a dynamic, interactive system tailored to your specific needs.
